Sponsor a Participant

For £15 a month, you can stand with local people in Clydebank on a 2-year journey from addiction to lasting freedom.

Become a Prayer and Sponsorship Partner

Prayer and sponsorship partners are vital in our fight against drug and alcohol misuse. We believe prayer unlocks prison doors and breaks the chains of addiction.

Your sponsorship supports participants through our 2-year, community-based recovery programme. It helps towards:

  • breakfast and lunch for every group member
  • course materials and resources
  • the salaries of our dedicated recovery workers
  • the day-to-day running costs of the programme

£15 / month

Sponsor a participant

Prefer to give once? Make a one-off gift

How Sponsorship Works

  1. 1

    You give £15 a month

    A simple monthly gift that you can pause or cancel at any time.

  2. 2

    It’s shared across the group

    Your gift supports the whole group rather than one named person.

  3. 3

    Your support carries on

    If someone moves on and someone new joins, your sponsorship carries straight on with the group.

What to Expect

  • Quarterly updates

    News on how the group is getting on, every three months.

  • Prayer requests

    Shared by group members, with their permission, as they journey from addiction to lasting freedom.

  • Belonging and purpose

    A real sense of belonging, knowing you’re part of something that matters.

Your sponsorship is helping to restore children to their parents, bring mums and dads back to their sons and daughters, and bring healing to every family affected by addiction.

Proof It Works

Civic & Parliamentary Recognition

A motion tabled in Parliament by Douglas McAllister MP recognises the work of Hope for Recovery and Hope Community Church in our community.

Proven Multi-Year Pathway

Typically 12–15 people attend each week, working through a structured 2-year programme (six 12-week courses), with 3 new graduates completing the pathway this November.
