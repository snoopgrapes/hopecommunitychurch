# Changes in this version

## New
- **support-us.html:** a Support Us hub (give online, give food, leave a gift in your will, give time and prayers), with a questions form.
- **css/site.css:** visible keyboard focus, skip link styling, and respect for "reduce motion" settings.
- **css/tailwind.css:** the styling library is now a small file served from your site instead of the development script from a CDN.

## Site-wide
- "Donate" and "Partner" buttons now read **Support Us** and go to the hub. Appeals read "Give Today".
- Page titles, meta descriptions, canonical links and social share tags on every page. Church structured data on the home page.
- Skip link, a single `<main>` landmark, labelled menus and one heading level 1 per page.
- Consistent footer on every page: Services, Recovery, Contact, Support Us, Prayer, Privacy, Terms.
- Unused DonorBox widget script removed.

## Home page
- New hero text and three buttons: Find Support, Visit Us This Sunday, Support Us.
- Graduation banner for 4 December 2026 and a "Find Support in Clydebank" section led by a Hope for Recovery card.
- Our History moved below the weekly services. Repeated "What to Expect" card headings made unique.

## Other fixes
- Café page: removed a duplicated block of menu links that showed under the header.
- Hope for Recovery page: added the missing `#structured-courses` and `#graduation` anchors.
- Dead `#contact` links on the Rehab, Street Outreach and Walk & Talk pages now go to the Contact page.

- Videos page: embeds use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com address.
- Privacy policy: now covers DonorBox, Buy Me a Coffee, YouTube, Google Fonts, prayer requests, cookies and the ICO, with a new date. Terms page updated to match. PayPal "Give now" links replaced.
